A trip here should feel like you have people on the island.

Our private services grew out of a question guests kept asking after their tour ended: can you help with the rest of the week? So we built it with the same people we already trust: the chefs, guides and therapists we would call for our own families.

Everything happens where you are staying or somewhere we know well, at a pace you set. You tell us the dates and the address, and we handle the rest, including the parts you would rather not think about.

How It Works

Four messages, more or less.

  1. 01

    Tell us the basics

    Where you are staying, how many of you, and which days you have. A WhatsApp message is enough to start.

  2. 02

    We shape it

    We come back with options, a plan for those dates and a written quote. Nothing you have to squeeze yourself into.

  3. 03

    You confirm

    Once you approve, we lock in the chef, guide or therapist and hold the times for you.

  4. 04

    We show up

    Arrival, setup and cleanup are ours. You do not coordinate anyone on your vacation.

Good To Know

Before you write to us.

Where we go

Island-wide. Travel time is part of the plan, so tell us the town or the hotel and we will tell you what makes sense from there.

How far ahead

Earlier is better, especially for chefs and holidays. Same-week requests are still worth asking about.

What it costs

Every quote is written for your dates, your group size, the location and the menu. Ask and you get the full number in writing.

Worth mentioning

Allergies, dietary preferences, mobility needs, birthdays, anniversaries and surprises. All of it changes how we plan.
