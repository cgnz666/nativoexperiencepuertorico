Good To Know

Where we go

Island-wide. Travel time is part of the plan, so tell us the town or the hotel and we will tell you what makes sense from there. The mobile bar is the exception: main island only, no Vieques or Culebra, and travel is included around San Juan with a flat charge by town everywhere else.

How far ahead

Earlier is better, especially for chefs and holidays. Same-week requests are still worth asking about.

What it costs

The mobile bar packages are flat price: what you see is what you pay. For the other four services, every quote is written for your dates, your group size, the location and the menu. Ask and you get the full number in writing.

Worth mentioning

Allergies, dietary preferences, mobility needs, birthdays, anniversaries and surprises. All of it changes how we plan.

Send your dates, the hotel or address, and how many of you there are. We come back with a plan and a written quote.
