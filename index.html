Difficulty: Challenging Includes a river hike
Wellness travel Built around how you feel, not how many places you see. Cold river water, warm hot springs and a slower rhythm, so you leave refreshed and recharged.

Forest yoga, riverside breathwork, cool natural pools and restorative hot springs in the Puerto Rican countryside.

What to expect
  • Pickup included We pick you up.
  • Forest yoga Yoga and mindful movement in a lush forest.
  • River hike A walk to a secluded river for breathwork and cold-water immersion. Expect to get wet.
  • Meal in Guavate An authentic meal that celebrates Puerto Rican flavors.
  • Hot springs End in the soothing warmth of Puerto Rico’s historic hot springs.

Caguas, Guavate, Ponce, Rincón & Isabela Day Trip

Difficulty: Easy Mostly by vehicle, short walks
Easy-pace pick An easy way to see the whole island, made for older travelers and anyone who prefers to take it slow. Most of the day is a scenic ride, with short, unhurried stops.

The best of Puerto Rico in one day, from historic Caguas and Ponce to the scenic west coast of Rincón and Isabela.

What to expect
  • Pickup included From the pickup point you choose.
  • Mostly on the road A long scenic drive around the island, with stops chosen along the way.
  • Light walking Leisurely strolls, like the downtown plaza in Ponce.
  • The stops Tobacco and art museums in Caguas, Ponce’s plaza and its famous ice cream, empanadas at the Rincón lighthouse and the surf town of Isabela. If a site is closed that day, we swap in another.
  • Optional Serrallés Castle and its Japanese garden in Ponce, $10.00 entrance.

Beyond La Perla

Difficulty: Moderate On foot, in the Caribbean heat
Culture and history With its origins as a refuge for freed slaves, La Perla grew into a symbol of resilience and Puerto Rican identity. On Fridays, the tour includes live bomba.

Feel the history, music, resilience and everyday life of one of San Juan's most recognized communities.

What to expect
  • Meeting point Meet your guide in Old San Juan.
  • On foot A walking tour through the neighborhood with a local guide.
  • Caribbean heat It’s a warm walk. Bring water, a hat and comfortable shoes.
  • Along the way Colorful streets, local art, Atlantic viewpoints and the spots from Despacito, with snacks and drinks.

420-Friendly Smoke Culture Experience

Island underground A relaxed, judgment-free look at the smoke scene, with personal recommendations from a local guide and people who share the vibe.

Explore Puerto Rico’s laid-back smoke culture through local shops, cigar traditions, 420-friendly spaces and community.

What to expect
  • Pickup included We pick you up.
  • Four shops Melaza, Fabricated Lifestyles, High Society and Smoke It, in Old San Juan and Miramar.
